How the estimate works

Your settings stay in this browser.

Annual value

Multiply each benefit’s annual use by what an equivalent benefit is worth to you. Add those amounts. Every value is your own judgement and can be edited.

Value compared with cost

Subtract the total annual cost, including any Programmes, from the estimated value. Divide the difference by that cost and multiply by 100. The percentage compares your estimated benefit value with the fee. It leaves out your time and any commercial result, so treat it as a value-to-fee comparison. A zero cost gives a net value only.

What to count

Count each benefit once, and only when you received something useful. A Challenge counts when you get usable analysis or next steps; submitting a question is not enough. For 2028 membership, the Programme row covers its Sprint, course and online events, so leave those out of the other rows.

Standard and cautious values

The calculator opens with cautious values and light use, so the first result is a conservative one. The cautious starting values are network access €300 a year, Sprint €600, expert sessions and webinars €90, archive results €30, and Task Force sessions €0 until you identify a specific benefit. The standard values are an alternative illustrative scenario. Challenge and Programme values are the same in both. When estimated value falls short of the fee, the calculator shows a negative result.

What the peer network is worth to you

The €300 cautious value equals €25 a month, or three hours a year saved in finding relevant expertise at €100 an hour. The €600 standard value equals €50 a month, or six such hours. Use your own hours and hourly cost. The value depends on reaching relevant people who respond, share candid experience and connect you across companies and functions. Admission screening on its own adds little. Set access to €0 if you would not pay for it.

The member mix is the practical reason. A supplier’s commercial director, a customer-side procurement lead and a sustainment manager can each show a different consequence of the same contractual decision. The €600 holds when you can reach those perspectives and use them.

Useful peer introductions

A useful introduction has three parts: a member connects you with someone who has relevant experience, you both agree to talk, and the conversation helps with a specific need. For example, an SME founder meets a procurement peer who explains a prime’s onboarding process, or a programme leader meets a sustainment specialist who has dealt with a similar supplier problem.

Count one completed, relevant conversation per new connection. A directory entry, an accepted connection request or a repeat chat with the same person does not count. The starting value is €0 because network access already covers most of this benefit. Enter a value only for a distinct gain beyond access, such as the time or cost of finding the same help elsewhere. Commercial wins and revenue from a connection stay outside this estimate.

How the other starting values were set

  • Sprint, €600 cautious or €900 standard: nine hours of facilitated work on members’ own cases, or about €67 and €100 an hour. The standard value sits within published individual prices for specialist professional courses. Employer group packages can cost less per person.
  • Programme, €1,200 each: the 2028 add-on price for a Sprint, a course and online events. Members from 2028 can add up to four a year. Each one adds €1,200 to your cost, so add only the Programmes you will follow.
  • Live Challenge, €150: at the lower end of published hourly rates for individual coaching. A peer response differs in scope from a contracted coach, so count it only when you receive a useful result.
  • Task Force, €0 cautious or €100 standard a session: count learning, feedback or a usable result you receive. Your own contribution to building a method is time you give, so it carries no value here.
  • Expert session, €90 cautious or €50 standard: the value depends on depth. An interactive specialist session or workshop with room for questions earns the higher figure. Use less for a general briefing or a recording.
  • Archive result, €30 cautious or €40 standard: about thirty minutes saved at €60 or €80 an hour. Enter a lower value if a resource saves you less time.

Membership model used

The 2027 entry year includes one Sprint; later seats depend on availability. From 2028, members can add one to four Programmes a year at €1,200 each, on top of membership. Each includes a Sprint, a course and online events, and counts only when added. ExecFrontline PRO is included for Founding Members, with its contents still being defined, so it carries €0 in this model.

Every benefit value here is an editable planning assumption. Task Force and event quantities depend on what is available. The 48-hour Challenge response is a target under validation. Other fees apply to a stated year. Member feedback on help received, time saved and outputs used will show how well these assumptions hold.
